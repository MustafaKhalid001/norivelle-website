Careers FAQs

Everything you need to know about joining the team.

1. Do you offer fully remote work options?

Yes! While we have a headquarters in Lahore, many of our roles, specifically in development and writing, are open to top-tier global talent working fully remotely.

2. What does the hiring process look like?

Our process is streamlined: A 30-minute culture fit interview, followed by a paid 2-hour technical assessment, and a final meeting with the CEO to discuss compensation and onboarding.

3. How would you describe the company culture?

Fast-paced, highly collaborative, and incredibly supportive. We leave our egos at the door and focus on data, creative problem solving, and celebrating wins together.

4. Do you offer internship opportunities?

Yes, we run a competitive 3-month paid internship program twice a year. High-performing interns are frequently offered full-time roles upon completion of the program.

5. Is there room for career progression?

Absolutely. We heavily prioritize promoting from within. As the agency scales, we regularly transition junior specialists into management and directorial roles.

6. What tech stack do your developers use?

Our current ecosystem heavily utilizes modern PHP, React, Node.js, and Liquid (for Shopify). We are always open to adopting new technologies if they prove to be more efficient.

7. I don't see an open position for my skills, can I still apply?

Yes! We are always on the lookout for exceptional talent. Feel free to email your resume and portfolio to our careers email with an open application, and we will keep you on file.
