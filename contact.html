Contact & Booking FAQs

What happens after you hit submit.

1. What is your standard communication response time?

We pride ourselves on lightning-fast communication. If you submit a form or send an email during operational hours, you will receive a response within 2 hours.

2. Can I walk into the Lahore HQ without an appointment?

While we love meeting clients in person, we kindly request that you schedule a consultation booking prior to arriving so we can ensure the right strategists are available to speak with you.

3. How do project kick-offs work?

Once an agreement is signed, we immediately schedule a video/in-person Kick-Off Call. We introduce you to your Account Manager, review the timeline, and collect any remaining assets we need to begin.

4. What happens during a free consultation booking?

It is a strictly no-pressure 30-minute discovery call. We ask questions about your business goals, identify current bottlenecks in your marketing, and determine if our agency is the right fit to scale your brand.

5. Is my file attachment secure?

Yes, any files uploaded through our multi-attachment portal are securely encrypted. We treat client data and proprietary brand assets with the utmost confidentiality.

6. Do I need to know exactly what services I need before contacting you?

Not at all! You can check multiple boxes or none at all. Our strategy team is highly skilled at auditing your current setup and recommending the specific services that will yield the highest ROI.

7. Can I request a formal proposal via the contact form?

Yes, please mention that you need a formal proposal in the "Your Message" field, and provide as much detail as possible about your project requirements so we can quote accurately.
