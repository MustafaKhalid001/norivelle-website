Service Deliverables FAQs

Detailed answers regarding timelines, technicalities, and what you get.

1. Do you use WordPress templates or custom builds?

While we can modify premium templates if requested for strict budgets, we heavily specialize in fully custom, from-scratch WordPress builds to ensure maximum performance and unique branding.

2. How long does a Shopify store build take?

A standard Shopify eCommerce build takes between 3 to 6 weeks depending on the number of products, custom app integrations required, and the complexity of the design.

3. When will I see results from SEO?

SEO is a long-term strategy. Typically, you will start seeing noticeable upward momentum in search rankings by Month 3, with significant ROI and traffic spikes occurring between Months 6 and 9.

4. What video formats do you deliver?

We deliver in any requested format (MP4, MOV, etc.) and optimize aspect ratios perfectly for the intended platform (16:9 for YouTube, 9:16 for TikTok/Reels, 1:1 for Instagram feeds).

5. Are your articles written by AI or humans?

All content is strictly written, edited, and fact-checked by human copywriters. We only use AI tools for backend processes like bulk keyword clustering or topic ideation, never for the final drafted copy.

6. What social media platforms do you manage?

We actively manage profiles on Instagram, Facebook, LinkedIn, X (Twitter), TikTok, and Pinterest. We tailor our platform selection based on where your target demographic actually spends their time.

7. Can I request revisions on design or video edits?

Absolutely. We include standard revision rounds (usually 2-3) in our service agreements to ensure the final deliverable matches your exact vision before we launch.
